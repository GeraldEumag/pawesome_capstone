## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 About this manual

This manual explains how to use Pawesome, step by step. It is written for customers and staff. You do not need any technical background to follow it.

### 1.2 What Pawesome does

Pawesome is a pet care management system. Customers use it to register their pets and book services such as grooming, veterinary appointments, and pet hotel stays. Staff use it to review bookings, take payments, manage stock, record medical care, and handle reports and payroll.

### 1.3 Who can use it

Pawesome has separate workspaces for these roles:

- **Customer** — books services and manages pets.
- **Receptionist** — reviews bookings, handles walk-in customers, and manages schedules.
- **Cashier** — takes payments and makes sales at the point of sale.
- **Inventory** — manages products, stock, and suppliers.
- **Veterinary** — handles appointments and medical records.
- **Manager** — reviews reports and handles staff attendance, leave, and payroll.
- **Admin** — manages user accounts and system settings.
- **Super Receptionist** — combines the receptionist, cashier, and inventory roles.

What you can see and do depends on the role of your account. If a page is missing or you are told you have no access, ask your administrator.

### 1.4 What you need

- The Pawesome website address, given to you by your administrator.
- An account with a username and password. Customers can create their own; staff accounts are created by the administrator.
- A computer or mobile device with a modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari).

### 1.5 Keeping your account safe

- Use only your own account. Do not share your password.
- Do not put passwords or customer information in screenshots or shared files.
- Check that you are on the right page before saving or submitting anything.
- Sign out when you are done, especially on a shared computer.

## 3. Getting started

### 3.1 Sign in

**Purpose:** Open your Pawesome workspace.

1. Open the Pawesome website address in your browser.
2. Select **Log In** if the login page is not already showing.
3. Enter your **Username** and **Password**.
4. Select **Sign In**.

**Expected result:** You arrive at the workspace for your role. New customers who have not verified their email are sent to the email verification page first.

**Note:** If you see an error, check your username and password and try again. If you forgot your password, see Section 3.3.

**Screenshot:** Figure UM-001 — Login page (screenshots/UM-001-login.png).

### 3.2 Create a customer account

**Purpose:** Register for a customer account.

1. On the Pawesome home page, select **Register**.
2. Fill in **Personal Information** — your first name, last name, and email address. The other fields (middle name, suffix, date of birth, contact number) are optional.
3. Continue to **Account Setup**. Choose a username and a password, then type the password again to confirm it.
4. Complete the **Emergency Contact** step.
5. Review your details, then accept the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
6. Submit the form.
7. Check your email and follow the verification link before making bookings.

**Expected result:** Your account is created and a verification email is sent to your address. See Section 3.3 for what to do next.

### 3.3 Verify your email and reset a forgotten password

**Verify your email:**

1. Open the verification email sent to your address.
2. Select the link in the email. It stays valid for 60 minutes.
3. Sign in when asked.

If the email does not arrive, check your spam folder or request a new link from the verification page.

**Reset a forgotten password:**

1. On the login page, select **Forgot Password**.
2. Enter your registered email address and submit.
3. Open the reset email and select the link within 60 minutes.
4. Enter a new password and confirm it.
5. Sign in with your new password.

**Expected result:** After verification you can make bookings. After a password reset you can sign in with the new password.

**Note:** The sign-in and reset pages never say whether an email is registered. This is normal and protects your account.

### 3.4 Sign out

Select **Logout** or **Log Out** in the side menu. Confirm if asked. Always sign out on shared computers.

## 4. Customer guide

The customer menu contains: **Services**, **My Pets**, **My Requests**, **Notifications**, **History**, **Medical**, **Reports**, **Profile**, and **Log Out**.

### What the booking statuses mean

| Status | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Pending | Staff are reviewing your request. | Wait for a decision. |
| Ongoing | Your booking is approved or the service is in progress. | Bring your pet at the scheduled time. |
| Pickup | Your pet is ready to go home. | Collect your pet. |
| Done | The service is finished. | Nothing needed. |
| Cancelled | The booking was cancelled. | Book again if needed. |

| Payment status | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Not paid | No payment has been made yet. | Pay online or at the counter. |
| Verifying payment | Your payment proof is being checked. | Wait for the cashier. |
| Paid | Payment is confirmed and a receipt was issued. | Keep the receipt number. |
| Payment rejected | The proof you sent could not be accepted. | Read the reason and send corrected proof. |
| Partly paid | Part of the amount is still due. | Settle the remaining balance. |
| Refunded | Your payment was returned. | Contact staff if unsure why. |

### 4.1 Add or manage a pet

**Purpose:** Keep your pet's information ready for bookings.

1. Open **My Pets**.
2. To add a pet, select **Add Pet** (or **Add your first pet** if the list is empty).
3. Fill in the pet's details and save.
4. To change a pet, select **Edit**.
5. To remove a pet from the active list, select **Archive**. To bring it back, open **Archived Pets** and select **Restore Pet**.
6. To see past records for a pet, select **Medical History** or **View History**.

**Expected result:** The pet appears in your active list and can be chosen when booking.

**Note:** Archived pets cannot be used for new bookings until you restore them.

**Screenshot:** Figure UM-003 — My Pets (screenshots/UM-003-customer-pets.png).

### 4.2 Book a grooming or veterinary service

**Purpose:** Request a service for one of your pets.

**Before you start:** Sign in with a verified account and make sure your pet is registered and active in **My Pets**.

1. Open **Services**.
2. Find the service you want and select **Book Now**.
3. In the booking form, choose your pet and the specific service.
4. Pick a date and an available time.
5. Add notes or describe your pet's concern.
6. Check the booking details and the price on the review screen.
7. Select **Go back** to fix anything, or **Confirm booking** to send the request.
8. Open **My Requests** to see the new request.

**Expected result:** Your request appears in **My Requests** with the status *Pending*. Staff will review it.

**Note:** If a time slot is taken, the form will tell you — pick another slot. Bookings are blocked until your email is verified.

**Screenshots:** Figure UM-004 — booking review before sending (screenshots/UM-004-booking-review.png); Figure UM-004a — filled grooming form (screenshots/UM-004a-grooming-form-filled.png); Figure UM-004b — request shown after sending (screenshots/UM-004b-grooming-submitted.png).

### 4.3 Book a Pet Hotel stay

**Purpose:** Reserve a boarding stay for your pet.

**Before you start:** Have a photo or scan of your pet's vaccination card ready to upload.

1. Open **Services**, find **Pet Hotel**, and select **Book Now**.
2. Select **New Reservation**.
3. Choose your pet and the **Stay Date** (check-in and check-out are on the same day).
4. Choose a room if one is offered.
5. Add notes if needed, and attach the **Vaccination Card** file.
6. Review the booking and price, then select **Submit Boarding Request** and confirm.
7. Track the request under **My Requests**.

**Expected result:** Your reservation appears as *Pending*. The receptionist checks the vaccination card before approving the stay.

**Note:** A vaccination card is required — the receptionist cannot approve a hotel booking without it.

[Insert screenshot of the Pet Hotel reservation form showing the vaccination card upload.]

### 4.4 Track requests and pay online

**Purpose:** Check your bookings and pay for them online.

1. Open **My Requests**. Use the tabs (**All**, **Pending**, **Ongoing**, **Pickup**, **Done**, **Cancelled**) to find a booking.
2. To pay, select **Pay now** on the booking.
3. Choose **GCash** or **Maya**, enter the payment reference number, and upload your proof of payment (jpg, png, or pdf, up to 5 MB).
4. Submit and wait — the payment shows *Verifying payment* while the cashier checks it.
5. If the payment is rejected, read the reason shown and select **Resubmit payment** with corrected details.

**Expected result:** Once the cashier confirms the payment, the booking shows *Paid* with a receipt number.

**Note:** Only send real payments through real GCash or Maya transactions. Do not upload someone else's proof.

**Screenshot:** Figure UM-015 — My Requests list (screenshots/UM-015-customer-request-paid.png).

### 4.5 A note about the store

Product browsing and customer checkout in the online store are not available in this version. You can still book and pay for services as described above. For product purchases, ask the staff at the counter.

### 4.6 Other customer pages

- **Notifications** — messages about your bookings and account. You can also change your email notification settings here.
- **History** — your past bookings and transactions.
- **Medical** — your pet's medical records from the clinic (Figure UM-011, screenshots/UM-011-customer-medical.png).
- **Reports** — summaries of your activity.
- **Profile** — your account details and profile photo. If you do not upload a photo, the system shows your initials.

## 5. Receptionist guide

The receptionist menu contains: **Booking** (Hotel, Veterinary, Grooming), **Manage Services**, **Walk-ins**, **Customer Profile**, **Chatbot**, **Live Chat**, **History**, **Reports**, **My Payroll**, **Profile**, and **Logout**.

### 5.1 Review and decide on a booking

**Purpose:** Check a customer's request and approve or reject it.

1. Open **Booking**, then choose **Hotel**, **Veterinary**, or **Grooming**.
2. Use the search or the status filter to find the request.
3. Open it and review the pet, owner, service, schedule, and any attached files.
4. For Pet Hotel requests, check and verify the vaccination card first — approval is not allowed without it.
5. For veterinary requests, choose the veterinarian when asked.
6. Select **Approve** to accept, or **Reject** and type the reason.
7. Reopen the record to confirm the new status.

**Expected result:** The booking moves to *Approved* or *Rejected*, and the customer is notified.

**Note:** Always give a clear reason when rejecting — the customer sees it.

**Screenshot:** Figure UM-005 — grooming booking queue (screenshots/UM-005-receptionist-grooming-queue.png).

### 5.2 Add a walk-in customer

**Purpose:** Book a service for a customer who is physically at the shop.

1. Open **Walk-ins**.
2. Select the service and fill in the customer, pet, and service details.
3. Review the details and confirm.
4. Approve the new booking so the customer can pay at the counter.

**Expected result:** The booking is created and the cashier can collect a cash payment for it.

## 6. Cashier guide

The cashier menu contains: **Dashboard**, **POS**, **Transactions**, **History**, **Reports**, **My Payroll**, **Profile**, and **Logout**.

### 6.1 Check a customer payment

**Purpose:** Confirm or reject a payment for a service booking.

1. Open **POS**, then **Payment Approvals**.
2. Find the request and review the reference, amount, and payment method.
3. Select **View Proof** to check the uploaded receipt or screenshot.
4. Choose the right action:
   - **Verify** — the online payment (GCash or Maya) checks out.
   - **Collect Payment** / **Collect Cash** — the customer is paying cash at the counter. Use this only for walk-in bookings, or for online bookings whose proof was rejected.
   - **Reject** — the proof is wrong or unreadable. Type the reason.
5. Confirm in the dialog.

**Expected result:** A verified or collected payment marks the booking *Paid* and produces a receipt number. A rejected payment goes back to the customer with your reason.

**Note:** Do not record an online payment as cash while its proof is still waiting — verify or reject the proof instead.

**Screenshot:** Figure UM-006 — Payment Approvals with a pending GCash payment (screenshots/UM-006-cashier-payment-approvals.png).

[Insert screenshot of the reject dialog showing the reason field.]

### 6.2 Make a sale at the POS

**Purpose:** Sell products or services over the counter.

1. Open **POS**.
2. Browse or search the item tabs (**All Items**, **Accessories**, **Health & Meds**, **Pet Food**, **Services**) and add items to the cart.
3. Check the quantities and the total.
4. Choose the customer, or leave it as **Walk-in Customer**.
5. Continue to payment, choose the payment method, and enter the amount received.
6. Complete the sale.
7. Review the receipt. Past sales are under **Transactions** and **History**.

**Expected result:** The sale is recorded, a receipt is produced, and stock counts update.

Open **Transactions** or **History** to review past sales — filter by date or search for a receipt. Use **Reports** for sales summaries.

## 7. Inventory guide

The inventory menu contains: **Dashboard**, **Stock Management**, **Suppliers**, **History**, **Barcode Generator**, **Reports**, **Monthly Audit**, **Audit Reports**, **Audit Analytics**, **My Payroll**, **Profile**, and **Logout**.

### 7.1 Find an item and check stock

1. Open **Stock Management**.
2. Search by name, or filter by category, brand, supplier, or status (**In Stock**, **Low Stock**, **Out of Stock**).
3. Select **View Info** on an item for details, or **History** for its stock movements.
4. Use **Reports**, **History**, and the audit pages to review records.

**Screenshot:** Figure UM-007 — Stock Management (screenshots/UM-007-inventory-stock.png).

### 7.2 Update an item or its stock

**Purpose:** Change item details or correct the stock count.

1. Find the item in **Stock Management**.
2. Select **Edit** to change item details, or **Adjust Stock** to fix the count.
3. Enter the values and the reason for the change.
4. Review and save, then confirm the movement in **History**.

**Note:** **Adjust Stock** can only reduce a count or correct it to a counted value. To add stock, record it as a new received batch instead — this keeps batch and expiry records correct.

**Expected result:** The item shows the updated details or count, and the change appears in History.

### 7.3 Suppliers, barcodes, and stock audits

- **Suppliers** — add and review supplier records.
- **Barcode Generator** — create barcodes for items.
- **Monthly Audit** — record a physical stock count.
- **Audit Reports** and **Audit Analytics** — review past counts and variances.

**Note:** Audits and stock changes affect inventory records. Double-check item, count, and reason before saving.

## 8. Veterinary guide

The veterinary menu contains: **Dashboard**, **Appointments**, **Customer Profiles**, **Current Boarders**, **Services**, **History**, **Reports**, **My Payroll**, **Profile**, and **Logout**.

### 8.1 Handle an appointment

**Purpose:** See your appointments and record the consultation.

1. Open **Appointments**. The cards at the top show today's count, pending, approved, and completion rate.
2. Open the appointment and review the patient and service details.
3. Select **Start Consultation** when you begin.
4. Record the consultation: concern, examination, assessment, treatment plan, and vitals. Add prescriptions or items used where needed.
5. Choose **Needs Confinement** if the pet must stay at the clinic.
6. Select **Save Draft** to continue later, or **Finalize & Review Bill** to finish. The finalized record can no longer be edited — later changes go in as dated corrections.
7. Review the bill and send it for payment. The visit closes automatically once the payment is confirmed.

**Expected result:** The appointment moves through consultation to billing, and closes when the customer pays.

**Note:** Medical records are sensitive. Enter only real findings for the actual patient.

**Screenshots:** Figure UM-008 — appointments list (screenshots/UM-008-veterinary-appointments.png); Figure UM-016 — an approved appointment (screenshots/UM-016-vet-appointment-approved.png).

## 12. Staff self-service and the attendance kiosk

### 12.1 My Payroll, My Attendance, and My Leave

Staff roles have pages for their own records:

- **My Payroll** — view your payslips. Keep payslip details private.
- **My Attendance** — see your own time records and file a correction if a punch is missing or wrong.
- **My Leave** — file a leave request and check your balance.

A manager reviews correction and leave requests. Approved leave is deducted from your balance.

### 12.2 Attendance kiosk

The attendance kiosk is a shared screen where staff record their time in and out.

1. Open the kiosk screen.
2. Enter the kiosk PIN given by the manager.
3. Scan your employee barcode (or follow the punch method in use) to record time in or time out.

**Expected result:** Your attendance is recorded for the day.

**Note:** Once the day is complete, the kiosk will not accept more punches. If your punch is missing or wrong, file a correction under **My Attendance**.

**Screenshot:** Figure UM-013 — attendance kiosk sign-in (screenshots/UM-013-attendance-kiosk.png).

## 13. A note about the Payroll role

There is no separate Payroll Manager account. Payroll is handled by the Manager (Section 9.3), and every employee can view their own payslips under **My Payroll**. If you are asked to create a payroll-only account, check with the administrator first.

## 14. How a booking moves through the system

A typical online booking works like this:

1. **Customer** books a service and sends online payment proof.
2. **Receptionist** reviews the request and approves or rejects it.
3. **Cashier** checks the payment proof and confirms it — a receipt is issued.
4. **Staff** perform the service. For vet visits, the appointment closes after billing.
5. **Customer** watches the status under **My Requests** and keeps the receipt number.

Walk-in customers skip the online steps: the receptionist books them at the counter and the cashier collects cash.

## 15. Common problems and what to do

| Problem | What to try |
|---|---|
| Cannot sign in | Check for typing errors and that Caps Lock is off. Use **Forgot Password** if needed. If it still fails, your account may be inactive — ask the administrator. |
| Sent to the verification page | Open the verification email and select the link, or request a new one. |
| No verification or reset email | Check spam/junk folders. Request a resend. Make sure you typed the right email. |
| Cannot submit a booking | Confirm your email is verified, your pet is active in **My Pets**, and the time slot is still open. |
| Pet does not appear in the booking form | Add the pet in **My Pets** first, or restore it if it is archived. |
| Pet Hotel date or room unavailable | Choose another date or room — availability can change before you submit. |
| Hotel booking cannot be approved | A vaccination card must be attached and verified by the receptionist first. |
| Payment button missing | The booking may already be paid, or it may not be ready for payment yet. Check its status. |
| Payment rejected | Read the reason shown, then use **Resubmit payment** with a clearer or correct proof. |
| Product checkout not working | Online store checkout is not available in this version. Ask staff about in-store purchases. |
| Page missing or "access denied" | Your role does not include that page. Ask the administrator if you believe you should have access. |
| Something looks wrong or the site is down | Note the page and the time, do not resubmit, and report it to the staff or administrator. |

## 16. Keeping information safe

- Keep your password private and never share your account.
- Only open and change records your role is meant to handle.
- Do not put real payment details or personal information in test data, screenshots, or shared files.
- Payments, stock changes, medical records, user accounts, and payroll are permanent records — check before saving.
- If a submission's result is unclear, check the record first instead of submitting again.
- Sign out when finished, especially on shared devices.

## Matching and quality issues observed

- The explicit Rajkumar placeholder is not an acceptable reference.
- The Pressman entry appears to omit Bruce R. Maxim, and the Fitzpatrick entry omits Isha Gill.
- The Wei entry can be improved with its verified DOI; the NPC reference uses only the homepage.
- Searchable in-text author-year forms generally point to entries in the list, but complete citation matching and whether every reference is cited cannot be certified from a PDF text dump alone.
- A source's existence does not establish that it supports the sentence citing it. The full article/source and the corresponding manuscript passage must be checked before retaining a claim.

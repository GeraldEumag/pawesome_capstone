# Blocked Items and Required Inputs

Phase 1 cannot produce a safe revised manuscript until the following source materials and author confirmations are supplied. Please provide only research-authorized material and de-identify survey responses; respondent names, contact details, and personal identifiers are not needed for calculation.

## P1 - Required to begin editing the manuscript

1. **Complete editable thesis source**: the DOCX used to produce `PAPER_updated-2.pdf`, including embedded figures, all chapters, references, appendices, and table/figure lists. If maintained in another format, provide the actual source files and fonts/assets. Do not send another PDF-only copy.
2. **Confirm the approved title/version** and identify any tracked panel/adviser comments or a marked-up prior draft, especially for MS-02 problem-statement changes.
3. **Author/adviser contact or written confirmations** for choices that cannot be inferred from code: actual research design, participant-selection process, approved respondent categories, and any panel preference for the scheduling/monitoring replacement.

## P1 - Required to reconcile respondents and statistics

4. **Anonymized raw evaluation data**: one row per respondent, anonymous ID, category, and each item's response.
5. **Response disposition log**: submitted/complete/valid/duplicate/excluded counts and the pre-established exclusion criteria. Include non-identifying reasons only.
6. **Approved questionnaire and scoring codebook**: exact question wording, scale weights, reverse-coded items (if any), group mapping, missing-value rules, and interpretation thresholds.
7. **Original statistical workbook/script** used to produce the printed tables, abstract, summary, and conclusions.
8. **Sampling information**: recruitment method, actual selection steps, population/frame size if it existed, and the reason for including each participant category. Confirm whether purposive sampling was actually used. Do not apply Slovin unless its assumptions and required population information are documented and the adviser approves its use.

## P1/P2 - Required to complete source and figure revisions

9. **Rajkumar et al. (2024) source**: full authors, title, venue/publisher, publication details, DOI/URL, and the cited passage. If unavailable, the authors/adviser must decide whether to remove the citation and dependent claim.
10. **All cited source files or stable full-text links**, particularly sources used for local statistics, veterinary/public-health context, the conceptual framework, Agile/Incremental rationale, and claims in Chapter II.
11. **Thesis diagram source files** for the conceptual framework, DFDs, context, fishbone, ERD, architecture, flowchart, and original wireframes. Repository architecture files are not established as source files for every embedded thesis figure; one contains a Telegram Bot API box that conflicts with the current scope.
12. **Actual system screenshots in original form** and confirmation of which are implemented screenshots versus original design wireframes, so MS-20 can be completed without relabeling or fabricating wireframes.
13. **Project schedule source records**: dated plans, milestone/change logs, adviser/client review records, and actual start/end status. Also provide any panel/adviser instruction naming a preferred replacement for the Gantt chart. The printed chronology in the PDF is not independent source evidence.
14. **Evidence for historical development/deployment claims**: dated test reports, meeting/review notes, deployment/release records, and whether user training actually occurred. Do not state production deployment or training from repository configuration alone.
